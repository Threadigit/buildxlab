BuildxLab is a technology company working to make building and running a two-person business sustainable and repeatable. We build the tools, teach people how to use them, and put our approach to work through incubation. We also help organisations make the transition.

Our ambition

Technology carrying 80% of the work needed to build a product and a profitable business, with a company of no more than two people equipped with the right technology stack. That is the target we are working towards.

Technology

Build the technology.

We build software and hardware that take on more of the work involved in creating products and running businesses.

Our longer-term plan is to own the AI tools and computing infrastructure behind that work.

Builder development

Teach people to build.

We train people to learn the ropes of building a product and bringing it to market. That includes using AI to research, prototype, build products, and run business workflows. Our approach starts with two complementary roles.
