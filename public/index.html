A technology company

Build products and businesses with up to two people.

BuildxLab is a technology company working to make that possible. We build the tools, teach people how to use them, and put our approach to work through incubation. We also help organisations make the transition.

Our ambition

Technology carrying 80% of the work needed to build a product and a profitable business, with a company of no more than two people equipped with the right technology stack. That is the target we are working towards.

Technology

Build the technology.

We build software and hardware that take on more of the work involved in creating products and running businesses.
